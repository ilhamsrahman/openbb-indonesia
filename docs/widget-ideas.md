Tier 2 — market-wide, one month at a time
3. Top foreign-owned stocks — rank all equities by foreign ownership % for a chosen month. Table or bar chart.
4. Top local-owned stocks — same idea, inverse.
5. Biggest movers — stocks with the largest foreign ownership % change month-over-month (who foreign investors are buying into / exiting).

Tier 3 — market-wide, across all months (macro trend)
7. Aggregate foreign flow — sum Foreign Total across all equities, per month → a single macro line: "is foreign money net flowing into or out of IDX." This is the single most-watched number in this kind of data.
8. Investor class concentration trend — total holdings by each investor type (e.g. total Mutual Fund AUM, total Pension Fund holdings) across the whole market, over time.
